Project 02 — Command Task Registry

FUA Tracker

Centralised tracking of follow-up actions from meetings and conferences. It lives in Teams — open it like any other tab.

Foundations built & tested — pilot next

The problem

01

Scattered everywhere

Actions end up in chat threads, inboxes, notebooks and someone's memory.

02

No live single status

Nobody can say at a glance what's done, what's late, or what's stuck.

03

Surfaces too late

Overdue items appear only when someone chases — or at the next meeting.

One register. One live status. One audit trail.

  • One place for every action
  • A status that's always current
  • A complete, tamper-proof record

How it works — one loop, end to end

LEADER RaiseA team leader logs the follow-up action, straight from the meeting decision.

You only ever type your own update. FUA Tracker keeps the score — the current status, the parent roll-up, and the full audit trail.

The four screens

SCREEN 01 · FOR TEAM LEADERS — a dashboard, not a launcher

  • AAt-a-glance KPIs. Open, due-soon, overdue and completed — the numbers that matter, up top.
  • BNeeds Attention. Overdue and blocked subtasks triaged into one list, so nothing slips.
  • CLive activity ledger. Every status change, straight from the immutable update log.

SCREEN 02 · FOR EVERYONE — your personal work queue

  • AFiltered to you. Only the actions assigned to you, grouped by status with live counts.
  • BUrgency at a glance. A red edge means overdue; amber means due within seven days; a brass tick flags high priority.
  • COne tap to update. Open any row to post progress — no hunting through lists.

SCREEN 03 · THE ONE THING YOU DO — the record keeps itself

  • AContext locked in. The task, its due date and the last entry sit right beside your input.
  • BStatus in, status out. Pick the new state; the transition is shown before you commit.
  • CAppend, never overwrite. Each update is a timestamped, immutable line in the audit trail.

SCREEN 04 · FOR TEAM LEADERS — a clear two-step work order

  • AStructured like a work order. Numbered sections: particulars, meeting context, priority & timeline.
  • BCan't commit half-done. A live checklist tracks required fields; commit stays locked until it's complete.
  • CAutomatic reference. The FUA number is assigned on commit — no manual numbering to get wrong.

Project 03

SmartCheck

A digital checklist system for recurring compliance and readiness checks — issued to phones, completed in the window, finalised automatically.

Prototype built — to be onboarded to MCC
3 roles 7 answer types 6 schedule patterns PDF + Excel reports All screens from the running app

Built for timed checks

BDO / CDO HOTO Vehicle NMT BOC Guard Commander HOTO

The scheduled life cycle — one window, start to finish

Window opensDeadline
T–120 T–60

Every window closes with an answer

Completed

Submitted in the window — redo any time before the deadline.

Missed

Flagged automatically when the window closes unsubmitted.

Skipped

Deliberately stood down — can be undone inside the window.

  • A reason is mandatory for missed or skipped checks; skips are excluded from completion rates.
  • Each submission is a new timestamped record — an append-only trail, never an edit.

What each role gets

Run a check PAGE BY PAGE

  • Only what is due now — the home screen lists checks whose window is open, with live countdowns in agency time (24-hour shifts spanning midnight included).
  • Seven answer types — Yes/No, number, photo, checkbox, dropdown, text, date & time — each with the right control, autosaving as you go.
  • Conditional logic — answering "No" on engine oil reveals the follow-up: litres of oil topped up.
  • No partial records — submit stays disabled until every item is answered; completion is collaborative, with per-question attribution.

Report an issue ESCALATION

  • Flip "Affects operational readiness" on any item to raise an issue right where the defect was found.
  • Up to five photos per issue (JPEG/PNG).
  • The issue stays linked to the item and submission, shows as a badge in the checklist, and is tracked until resolved.

Issue tracker OPEN / RESOLVED

  • Open and Resolved tabs across every check the team runs — no digging through old checklists.
  • Category, item, photo count, remarks — who raised it and when.
  • Update with new findings or resolve with a resolution label; resolved issues keep their history.
  • The bottom-nav badge counts open issues until the last one is cleared.

History & evidence APPEND-ONLY

  • Colour-coded calendar — green when everything was submitted, rust when something was missed: a month of compliance at a glance.
  • Daily PDF per check — submitter names, every answer, and embedded photos, generated on demand.
  • Monthly Excel export — one row per submission event, restarts and skips included, plus the template change log.

Dashboard LIVE DAY VIEW

  • Completion %, submitted count, open issues and misses for any date — step back through history with the day stepper.
  • Expand a team to see each check's state for that day — the same canonical colours as the checker UI.

Responses ANSWER LEVEL

  • Latest answer per check — pick a template and an item, see what every team answered last, side by side.
  • Attribution built in — who answered and when, for every row.
  • Problems stand out — "Worn · 1 open": issue flags sit right next to the answers that raised them.

Template builder NO CODE

  • Pages, sections and typed items, with descriptions and inline images.
  • Scoring & logic without code — per-option scores and conditional follow-ups ("when No, ask litres topped up") configured in place.
  • Draft until published; duplicate items, pages or whole templates; search across every template.

Schedules & notifications SIX PATTERNS

  • Daily, twice-a-day, weekly, monthly, one-time and open — windows live in agency time and may span midnight.
  • Change without losing history — pause, resume, transfer between teams, or edit mid-stream.
  • Deadline nudges: email at T–60, webhooks at T–120 for unsubmitted checks; instant alerts on new issues.
  • Test before you trust — fire a sample webhook payload with one click.
